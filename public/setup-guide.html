FAQ

Setup guide questions

Which setup guide should I follow first?

If you use a separate script, set up a Vanguard emulator first (OXA, Soyuz, NOI, Seraph or the LoL Vanguard Emulator), then follow your script guide. Bundle guides show the order for both keys. If you also use the Perm Spoofer, run it before everything else.

Where do I get the loader after purchase?

Some keys are shown on-screen and emailed right after checkout; for others you open a ticket in the LOLScript Discord with your order ID. Each product guide says which applies. The official loader link and update notes are shared in Discord.

Do I need more than one product?

No. Every product is sold separately. Most scripts need a Vanguard emulator, and US Tool Pro AIO and RS Pro AIO also need a Hanbot or Legend Sense key. Bundles include an emulator and a script in one order.

What should I do after a League of Legends patch?

Open the loader and follow the update prompt. Updates are normally ready the same day. You can check current compatibility on the status page.

Where can I get setup help?

Join the LOLScript Discord and open a support ticket with your order email and a short description of the step you are stuck on.
