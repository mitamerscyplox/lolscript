What changed

Vanguard in League of Legends

Riot brought Vanguard, its kernel-level anti-cheat, to League of Legends in 2024. Vanguard starts with Windows and runs below normal applications, which is a very different environment from the user-mode protection League used before.

For League of Legends scripts, this meant that the tools and loaders built for the old environment stopped working almost overnight. Many long-running public scripts shut down or moved to private access.
