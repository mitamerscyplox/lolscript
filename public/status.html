How is LoL patch compatibility checked?

The current League of Legends patch is checked daily against Riot's official version data. The result is shown on this page and in the site header as "Working on LoL Patch".

What happens when a new League of Legends patch releases?

Open the loader and follow the update prompt. Updates for the LoL Script are normally ready the same day, and patch notes are posted in the LOLScript Discord.

How fast are keys delivered?

Keys are delivered instantly after payment clears, on-screen and to the email used at checkout.

Where can I get urgent compatibility information?
