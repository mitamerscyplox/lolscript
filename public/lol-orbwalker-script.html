What it does

What a League of Legends orbwalker does

An orbwalker times movement commands between auto attacks. Instead of standing still after every attack, your champion moves during the window where movement does not cancel the attack, which keeps damage up while you kite forward or backward.

Attack timing

Moves between auto attacks at the right moment so attacks are not cancelled early or delayed.

Hotkeys

Orbwalker modes and default hotkeys

Each mode is held on a key, so you decide when the orbwalker is active.

  1. Space: Combo

    All-in mode. The orbwalker and champion combo logic work together on your selected target.

  2. V: Lane clear

    Clears minion waves and farms quickly.

  3. C: Harass

    Last hits minions and pokes enemy champions when they are in range.

  4. X: Last hit only

    Only last hits minions, without harassing enemies.
