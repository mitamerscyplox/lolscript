Loader configuration

Installation and configuration guide

Follow these loader steps in order for a healthy completion. Always run the loader as administrator.

Step 1

TPM cleanup

Type tpm.msc in the Windows search bar and press Enter. In the window that opens, click Clear TPM... on the right side. Your computer will restart automatically after this step.

Step 2

Network and system configuration

After the PC boots, right-click the loader and choose Run as administrator. Press 5 on your keyboard, then press Enter. Click Yes on the warning. Your internet may disconnect briefly — this is normal.

Step 3

Secondary system preparation

Run the loader as administrator again. Press 4, then press Enter. Click Yes on every warning. The PC will restart when the process finishes.

Step 4

Final confirmation and completion

After the PC boots, open the loader as administrator one last time. Press 1, then press Enter. Answer the three warnings in this order: Yes, No, Yes. The computer will restart one final time — setup is then complete.
