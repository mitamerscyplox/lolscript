MMR explained

What is League of Legends MMR?

MMR, or matchmaking rating, is the hidden value League of Legends uses to build fairer ranked games. Your visible rank shows your ladder position, while hidden MMR influences matchmaking and the LP you gain or lose after each match.

Why your LP gains change

If your MMR is above your visible rank, the ladder often rewards wins with stronger LP gains because it expects you to climb. If your MMR is below your visible rank, gains can shrink and losses can hurt more.

Why exact MMR is hidden

Riot's public API does not expose an exact hidden MMR number. Any LoL MMR checker should be treated as an estimate based on ranked tier, LP, match trend, and visible ranked performance.

How to raise your MMR

  • Play ranked consistently instead of swapping queues constantly.
  • Focus on a small champion pool and one or two roles.
  • Review losses where you gave up shutdowns, objectives, or tempo.
  • Avoid tilt queues after a heavy losing streak.
