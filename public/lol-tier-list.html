How to use this LoL tier list

A tier list should guide champion select, not replace judgment. A lower-tier comfort pick can still beat a high-tier champion if you know the matchup, play around item spikes, and understand your win condition.

  • Use S tier for the strongest meta picks and high-ban threats.
  • Use role filters when you only care about your lane or queue role.
  • Search a champion before a game to quickly judge current meta strength.

What affects champion rankings?

Champion strength changes with patch balance, item updates, pick rate, ban rate, win rate, team compositions, and how easy a champion is to execute in solo queue.

  • High win-rate niche picks are not always blind-pick safe.
  • Popular champions can look weaker when many inexperienced players use them.
  • Role flexibility matters because it creates stronger draft options.
