RS Pro AIO setup guide

Advanced add-on for Legend Sense and Hanbot with champion combos, optimized combat logic, Yuumi automation, and tuned evade. Follow the steps below after checkout. Your key and the official loader link arrive through a Discord ticket.

Preparation

Before you start

Check these points before installing RS Pro AIO. Use only the loader link sent by LOLScript support.

  1. Prepare your Windows PC

    Use an up-to-date Windows 10 or 11 PC with League of Legends installed and opening normally through the Riot Client.

  2. Get your key and loader

    After checkout, join lolscript.store/discord, open a ticket and send your order ID. Our team sends your key and the official loader link there.

  3. Have a Vanguard emulator ready

    RS Pro AIO loads while Riot Vanguard is not running. Set up an emulator first: OXA, Soyuz, NOI, Seraph.

  4. Install the base script

    RS Pro AIO is an add-on and needs an active Legend Sense or Hanbot key.

Installation

How to install RS Pro AIO

Follow these steps in order. Ask in your Discord ticket if a step does not match what you see.

Step 1

Have LS or Hanbot

RS Pro AIO runs on top of an active Legend Sense or Hanbot key.

Step 2

Start your Vanguard emulator

Run your Vanguard emulator, then load your script.

Step 3

Enable RS Pro AIO

Add the key in your script and load the profiles.

Before your first game
  • Only use the loader link sent in your LOLScript Discord ticket.
  • Start with default settings, then change one option at a time.
  • Test in practice tool or a custom game before ranked.
